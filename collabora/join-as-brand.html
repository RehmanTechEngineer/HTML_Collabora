Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about our platform

We have a rigorous verification process that includes checking the authenticity of followers, engagement rates, and content quality. We also verify the influencer's identity and professional history. This ensures that brands only work with legitimate creators who can deliver real results.

You can run various types of campaigns including sponsored content, product reviews, brand ambassadorships, account takeovers, giveaways, affiliate marketing, and event promotions. Our platform supports campaigns across all major social media platforms including Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Twitter, and more.

When you create a campaign, you'll set a budget and payment terms. Funds are held in escrow until the influencer completes the agreed deliverables. Once you approve the content, payment is released to the influencer. This ensures both parties are protected and satisfied with the collaboration.
