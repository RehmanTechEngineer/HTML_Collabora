Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about joining as an influencer

Yes, it's completely free to create an influencer profile on Collabora. We only take a small commission when you successfully complete paid collaborations with brands. There are no monthly fees or hidden charges.

We welcome influencers of all sizes, from nano-influencers (1K-10K followers) to mega-influencers (1M+ followers). What matters most is having an engaged, authentic audience and quality content. Many brands specifically look for micro and nano-influencers due to their higher engagement rates and niche audiences.

When you complete a collaboration, the brand releases payment through our secure payment system. You can withdraw your earnings via direct bank transfer, PayPal, or other payment methods. Payments are typically processed within 7 business days after campaign completion and approval.

Collabora partners with brands across various industries, including fashion, beauty, tech, food, travel, fitness, lifestyle, and more. All brands on our platform are verified and vetted to ensure they're legitimate and reliable partners for our influencers.

You have complete control over which brands you work with. You can review all collaboration offers and accept only those that align with your content, values, and audience. There's no obligation to accept any offer you receive.

We support all major social media platforms, including Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Twitter, Facebook, Pinterest, Twitch, and LinkedIn. You can connect multiple platforms to your profile to showcase your full online presence and reach.
