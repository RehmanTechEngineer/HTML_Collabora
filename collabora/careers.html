Our Interview Process

What to expect when you apply

1

Application Review

Our recruiting team reviews your application and resume. We aim to respond to all applications within one week.

2

Initial Screening

A 30-minute call with a recruiter to discuss your background, experience, and what you're looking for in your next role.

3

Skills Assessment

Depending on the role, you may be asked to complete a take-home assignment or technical challenge to showcase your skills.

4

Team Interviews

A series of interviews with team members and cross-functional partners to assess your skills, experience, and cultural fit.

5

Final Interview

A conversation with a senior leader or department head to discuss your career goals and how you can contribute to our mission.

6

Offer & Onboarding

If there's a mutual fit, we'll extend an offer and work with you to ensure a smooth onboarding experience.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about working at Collabora

Yes, we offer flexible work arrangements, including fully remote, hybrid, and in-office options. Many of our roles can be performed remotely from anywhere in the world, while some roles may require occasional in-person collaboration at one of our offices. Each job posting specifies the location requirements.

We offer a comprehensive benefits package that includes:
  • Competitive salary and equity options
  • Health, dental, and vision insurance
  • Flexible PTO policy
  • 401(k) matching (US) or equivalent retirement plans
  • Learning and development stipend
  • Home office setup allowance
  • Wellness programs and mental health support
  • Parental leave
  • Regular team events and retreats
Benefits may vary by location and employment status.

Our culture is collaborative, innovative, and impact-driven. We value transparency, diversity, and work-life balance. We celebrate wins together and learn from challenges. We encourage open communication and feedback at all levels of the organization. While we work hard to achieve our goals, we also prioritize wellbeing and fun. Our team members often describe our culture as supportive, dynamic, and purpose-driven.

Our hiring process typically takes 2-4 weeks from application to offer, though this can vary depending on the role and the number of applicants. We respect your time and aim to provide timely updates throughout the process. If you have any time constraints or are considering other opportunities, please let our recruiting team know so we can try to accommodate your timeline.

We're always interested in connecting with talented individuals who are passionate about our mission. You can send your resume to careers@Collabora.com with a note about your interests and skills. We'll keep your information on file and reach out if a suitable position becomes available. We also encourage you to follow us on LinkedIn for the latest job openings.
