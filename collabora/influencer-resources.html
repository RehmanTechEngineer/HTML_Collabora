Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about growing as an influencer

Determining your rates depends on several factors including your follower count, engagement rate, content quality, niche, and the deliverables requested. As a general benchmark, many influencers use the "$100 per 10K followers" rule as a starting point, but this varies widely. Check out our Rate Calculator tool and Pricing Guide for more detailed guidance based on your specific metrics.

Improving engagement requires a combination of strategies: create content that encourages interaction (questions, polls, calls to action), respond to comments promptly, post consistently at optimal times, create authentic and relatable content, use relevant hashtags, collaborate with other creators, and experiment with different content formats. Our Engagement Boosting Guide provides a comprehensive strategy for increasing meaningful interactions with your audience.

When approaching brands, research the company thoroughly and customize your pitch to show how your audience aligns with their target market. Include your key metrics, content examples, and specific collaboration ideas. Be professional, concise, and highlight the value you can provide. Our Brand Outreach Templates provide customizable email templates and follow-up strategies to help you secure partnerships.

A comprehensive media kit should include your bio and brand story, audience demographics and insights, engagement metrics, content categories and examples, past collaborations and testimonials, services and rates, and contact information. Keep it visually appealing and concise (typically 3-5 pages). Download our Media Kit Template for a professionally designed layout that you can customize with your information.
