Our Story

Founded in 2020, Collabora was born from a simple observation: influencer marketing was too complicated.

Our founders, Sarah Johnson and Michael Chen, experienced the challenges of influencer marketing firsthand—brands struggled to find the right influencers, while content creators had difficulty connecting with relevant brands.

They envisioned a platform that would streamline the entire process, from discovery to collaboration to measurement, all while ensuring authenticity and transparency.

Today, Collabora has grown into a global community of over 10,000 influencers and 5,000 brands, facilitating meaningful collaborations that drive real business results.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide everything we do

Authenticity

We believe in genuine connections between brands and influencers. We prioritize authentic content that resonates with audiences and drives meaningful engagement.

Transparency

We provide clear, data-driven insights for both brands and influencers. No hidden fees, no inflated metrics—just honest, actionable information.

Community

We're building more than a platform—we're creating a community where brands and influencers can learn, grow, and succeed together.

Innovation

We're constantly evolving our platform with cutting-edge technology to stay ahead of industry trends and provide the best possible experience.

Trust

We've built our platform on a foundation of trust, ensuring secure transactions, verified profiles, and reliable performance metrics.

Diversity

We celebrate diversity in all forms and are committed to creating an inclusive platform that represents voices from all backgrounds and communities.

Our Journey

How we've grown over the years

2020 - The Beginning

Collabora was founded in San Francisco with a mission to revolutionize influencer marketing. We launched with a beta version of our platform and 100 early adopters.

2021 - Rapid Growth

Secured $5M in Series A funding. Expanded our team to 25 employees and reached 1,000 influencers and 500 brands on the platform. Launched our mobile app.

2022 - Global Expansion

Opened offices in London and Singapore. Reached 5,000 influencers and 2,000 brands. Introduced advanced analytics and campaign management tools.

2023 - Industry Recognition

Named "Best Influencer Marketing Platform" at the Digital Marketing Awards. Secured $20M in Series B funding. Reached 8,000 influencers and 4,000 brands.

2024 - Innovation & Growth

Launched AI-powered matching algorithm. Surpassed 10,000 influencers and 5,000 brands. Expanded to 150+ countries. Introduced blockchain-based verification system.
