Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

We strive to respond to all inquiries within 24 business hours. For urgent matters, please indicate this in the subject line of your message, and we'll prioritize your request.

For technical support, please select "Technical Support" in the subject dropdown of our contact form. Alternatively, you can email support@Collabora.com with details of the issue you're experiencing, including screenshots if possible.

To schedule a personalized demo, please select "Partnership Opportunities" in the subject dropdown and mention that you're interested in a demo. One of our team members will reach out to arrange a convenient time.

You can view all current job openings on our Careers page. If you don't see a position that matches your skills but are still interested in joining our team, feel free to send your resume to careers@Collabora.com.

We take the safety and integrity of our platform seriously. To report inappropriate content or behavior, please select "Feedback" in the subject dropdown and provide details of the issue. Our trust and safety team will investigate promptly.
