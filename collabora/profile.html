About

Hi there! I'm Sophia, a fashion and lifestyle content creator based in Los Angeles. With over 5 years of experience in the industry, I specialize in sustainable fashion, home decor, and mindful living.

My content focuses on helping my audience make conscious choices while still looking stylish and feeling confident. I believe that fashion should be accessible, sustainable, and fun!

I've worked with numerous global brands to create authentic content that resonates with my engaged community. My audience is primarily women aged 25-34 who are interested in fashion, sustainability, and lifestyle content.
