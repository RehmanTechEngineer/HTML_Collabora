Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about influencer marketing

Influencer marketing budgets vary widely depending on your goals, industry, and the influencers you want to work with. Micro-influencers (10K-50K followers) might charge $200-$500 per post, while macro-influencers (100K-1M followers) might charge $1,000-$10,000+ per post. We recommend starting with a test budget to run a small campaign with a few influencers, then scaling based on performance. Our ROI Calculator can help you estimate potential returns based on your investment.

Success measurement depends on your campaign goals. Key metrics include engagement (likes, comments, shares), reach and impressions, website traffic, conversions (sales, sign-ups), brand sentiment, and ROI. Our platform provides comprehensive analytics to track these metrics, and our Campaign Report Template can help you organize and present your results effectively.

Finding the right influencers involves looking beyond follower count to factors like audience demographics, engagement rate, content quality, and brand alignment. Our platform offers advanced search filters, AI matching, and curated collections to help you find influencers who align with your brand values and can effectively reach your target audience. Check out our Influencer Selection Guide for a comprehensive approach to finding the perfect partners.

An effective influencer brief should include campaign objectives, key messaging points, content guidelines, deliverables and timeline, do's and don'ts, disclosure requirements, approval process, and compensation details. It should provide clear direction while allowing creative freedom. Our Campaign Brief Template provides a comprehensive framework that you can customize for your specific campaign needs.
