About Collabora

Basic questions about our platform

Collabora is a comprehensive influencer marketing platform that connects brands with social media influencers. Our platform streamlines the entire influencer marketing process, from discovery and outreach to campaign management and performance tracking. We provide tools for both brands looking to grow their presence through influencer partnerships and content creators seeking brand collaborations.

Collabora supports all major social media platforms, including Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Twitter, Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitch, and Pinterest. Our analytics tools integrate with these platforms to provide comprehensive performance metrics and audience insights for your campaigns.

We have a comprehensive verification process that includes authenticating social media accounts, reviewing content quality and engagement metrics, and checking for any history of fraudulent activity. We also continuously monitor influencer accounts to ensure they maintain authentic engagement and followers. Our verification badge indicates that an influencer has passed our vetting process and is a trusted member of our community.

Yes, Collabora is available worldwide. We have influencers and brands from over 150 countries on our platform. Our payment system supports multiple currencies, and our platform is available in English, Spanish, French, German, and Japanese, with more languages coming soon.

Getting started is easy! Simply sign up for an account, choose whether you're joining as a brand or an influencer, and complete your profile. Brands can then start searching for influencers, while influencers can set up their profiles to be discovered by brands. Our platform guides you through each step of the process, and our support team is available to help if you have any questions.
For Brands

Brand Questions

Information for businesses and agencies

Collabora offers several ways to find the perfect influencers for your brand:
  • Advanced Search: Use our search filters to narrow down influencers by platform, category, audience demographics, engagement rate, location, and more.
  • AI Matching: Our proprietary algorithm analyzes your brand profile and campaign goals to recommend influencers who are most likely to deliver results.
  • Collections: Browse our curated collections of influencers organized by industry, content style, and audience type.
  • Discovery Feed: Explore our personalized feed of influencers tailored to your brand's preferences and past collaborations.
You can also save your favorite influencers to lists for future reference and collaboration.

Collabora supports a wide range of campaign types, including:
  • Sponsored Content: One-time posts or videos featuring your product or service
  • Brand Ambassadorships: Long-term partnerships with influencers who regularly promote your brand
  • Product Reviews: Honest reviews of your products by influencers
  • Affiliate Marketing: Performance-based campaigns where influencers earn commission on sales
  • Giveaways and Contests: Promotions to engage the influencer's audience and increase brand awareness
  • Event Coverage: Influencers attending and covering your events
  • Content Creation: Hiring influencers to create content for your own channels
  • Takeovers: Influencers temporarily managing your social media accounts
Our platform provides templates and guidance for each campaign type to help you set clear objectives and deliverables.

Influencer marketing budgets vary widely depending on your goals, industry, and the influencers you want to work with. Micro-influencers (10K-50K followers) might charge $200-$500 per post, while macro-influencers (100K-1M followers) might charge $1,000-$10,000+ per post. Celebrity influencers with millions of followers can command $50,000+ per post.

We recommend starting with a test budget to run a small campaign with a few influencers, then scaling based on performance. Our platform provides rate benchmarks for different influencer tiers and content types to help you budget appropriately. You can also use our ROI calculator to estimate potential returns based on your investment.

Remember to factor in your Collabora subscription cost, content production expenses, and any additional promotional costs when planning your overall influencer marketing budget.

Collabora provides comprehensive analytics to measure the performance of your campaigns:
  • Engagement Metrics: Likes, comments, shares, saves, and overall engagement rate
  • Reach and Impressions: How many people saw the content
  • Audience Growth: New followers gained during the campaign
  • Traffic: Website visits generated through tracked links
  • Conversions: Sign-ups, downloads, or purchases attributed to the campaign
  • ROI: Return on investment based on campaign costs and results
  • Sentiment Analysis: The tone and sentiment of audience reactions
Our platform integrates with your website analytics, e-commerce platforms, and social media accounts to provide a holistic view of campaign performance. You can also create custom KPIs based on your specific goals and generate detailed reports to share with stakeholders.

Yes, we take compliance seriously. Our platform includes built-in disclosure reminders and guidelines to ensure that all influencer collaborations comply with FTC regulations and similar requirements in other countries. Our contract templates include clear language about disclosure requirements, and our campaign briefs remind influencers to properly disclose their partnerships.

We also provide educational resources about compliance best practices and monitor content to ensure proper disclosures are being used. However, it's ultimately the responsibility of both brands and influencers to ensure that all sponsored content is properly disclosed according to applicable laws and regulations.
For Influencers

Influencer Questions

Help for content creators

Joining Collabora as an influencer is free and straightforward:
  1. Sign up for an account and select "I'm an Influencer" during registration.
  2. Complete your profile with details about your content niche, audience demographics, and past collaborations.
  3. Connect your social media accounts to verify your following and provide analytics.
  4. Upload your best content samples to showcase your work.
  5. Set your collaboration preferences, including rates and the types of brands you want to work with.
Once your profile is complete, our team will review it for verification. This typically takes 1-3 business days. Once verified, your profile will be visible to brands searching for influencers, and you'll start receiving collaboration opportunities.

Determining your rates depends on several factors:
  • Audience Size: Your follower count across platforms
  • Engagement Rate: How actively your audience interacts with your content
  • Content Quality: The production value and creativity of your content
  • Niche: Some industries command higher rates than others
  • Exclusivity: Whether the brand wants exclusivity in their category
  • Usage Rights: How and where the brand can use your content
  • Content Type: Video content typically costs more than photos
As a general benchmark, many influencers use the "$100 per 10K followers" rule as a starting point, but this varies widely. Our platform provides rate benchmarks based on data from thousands of collaborations to help you price competitively.

You can set different rates for different content types in your profile, and you always have the opportunity to negotiate with brands before accepting a collaboration.

Collabora offers secure payment processing for all collaborations:
  1. When you accept a collaboration, the brand deposits the agreed payment into our secure escrow system.
  2. You create and publish the content according to the campaign brief.
  3. Once the brand approves the content, the funds are released to your account.
  4. You can withdraw funds to your preferred payment method (bank account, PayPal, etc.).
Payments are typically processed within 3-5 business days after withdrawal. For long-term collaborations, you can set up milestone payments to receive funds at different stages of the campaign.

We charge a 5% service fee on payments to cover payment processing and platform maintenance. This fee is transparent and clearly displayed before you accept any collaboration.

Absolutely. You have complete control over which brands you work with. When you receive a collaboration request, you can:
  • Accept: Agree to the collaboration as proposed
  • Negotiate: Suggest changes to the deliverables, timeline, or compensation
  • Decline: Politely decline the opportunity
We encourage you to only accept collaborations with brands that align with your values and content style. Maintaining authenticity is crucial for your audience trust and long-term success.

You can also set brand preferences in your profile to filter the types of collaboration requests you receive. For example, you can exclude certain industries or specify that you only want to work with eco-friendly brands.

Here are some tips to increase your visibility and appeal to brands:
  • Complete Your Profile: Fill out every section of your Collabora profile with detailed information.
  • Showcase Your Best Work: Upload high-quality content samples that demonstrate your style and capabilities.
  • Keep Your Analytics Updated: Regularly update your audience demographics and engagement metrics.
  • Set Clear Rates: Provide transparent pricing for different content types.
  • Be Responsive: Reply promptly to brand inquiries, even if you're declining.
  • Create a Media Kit: Upload a professional media kit that summarizes your value proposition.
  • Build Your Portfolio: Document past collaborations with case studies and results.
  • Engage with Brands: Follow and meaningfully engage with brands you'd like to work with.
We also offer resources and webinars on how to pitch to brands, create compelling content, and negotiate favorable terms. Check out our Influencer Resources section for more guidance.

Payment Questions

Billing and payment processing

For platform subscriptions, we accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover), PayPal, and bank transfers for annual plans.

For influencer payments, brands can fund their campaigns using credit cards, PayPal, or bank transfers. Influencers can withdraw their earnings via bank transfer, PayPal, or Stripe. We support payments in multiple currencies to accommodate our global user base.
